# Mission check (GDC Mission Maker Tools)

English | [Français](GDC_CheckMission.fr.md)

The **Check Mission** plugin checks the open mission world and reports what would break a game or deserves a look. It reads the world and never changes it.

## Usage

1. Open your mission world.
2. Run **Plugins > GDC > Check Mission**, or **Ctrl+Alt+C**.
3. Read the summary, then the details in the **Log Console**, filtered on `[GDC_CHECK]`.
4. Fix, then run it again with Ctrl+Alt+C or the **Check again** button.

## Settings

In **Plugins > Settings > Check Mission**:

| Setting | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Blocks (Mission Structure, Resources And Addons...) | the blocks of checks to run; an unchecked block is shown as skipped, never as passed |
| Skeleton Config | the mission skeleton block 1 compares the mission with: the same as in [Create Mission Skeleton](GDC_MissionSkeleton.md) |
| Show Info | shows the Info findings in the report or not; they are always counted in the result |
| Language | language of the dialogs and the report: English or French |

## Reading the report

Each finding gives:

- its **severity**: **error** (breaks the game, to fix), **warning** (may be intended, your call) or **info**;
- the number and title of the rule, with its **origin** in brackets: vanilla, GDC, or a framework (TILW, RL for Reforger Lobby);
- **Where**: the layer and the entity. Click the `@"ENTITY:..."` link to select the entity in the world;
- **Problem** and **Fix**;
- **Sheet**, when the rule has one: the address of its page in the knowledge base, to copy into the browser.

The rules of a framework only run when the mission uses it, that is when it places at least one of its entities. The header of the report shows the origins in use.

## The checks

The knowledge base explains the rules whose reason is not obvious: evidence, workaround, and when the rule may go. The title of these rules links to their sheet.
